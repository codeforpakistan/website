<p dir="ltr"><strong>About Code for Pakistan&nbsp;</strong></p><p dir="ltr">Imagine a Pakistan where technology strengthens communities, improves lives, and brings citizens and government closer together.</p><p dir="ltr">That’s the vision driving Code for Pakistan.</p><p dir="ltr"><a href="http://www.codeforpakistan.org/">Code for Pakistan</a> (CfP) is a civic innovation nonprofit that builds public interest technology and fosters inclusive citizen participation. Through a human-centered design process, we empower people to co-create digital solutions that address local challenges, make government more responsive, and create lasting social impact.</p><p dir="ltr">Our community of changemakers, technologists, designers, and researchers — across Pakistan and the diaspora — believes that technology, when shaped by the people it serves, can be a powerful force for the public good.</p><p dir="ltr">Over the past decade, CfP has completed eight cycles of its flagship&nbsp;<a href="https://codeforpakistan.org/programs/fellowships">Civic Innovation Fellowship Program</a>, introduced Pakistan’s first&nbsp;<a href="https://codeforpakistan.org/programs/internships">Women’s Civic Digital Internship Program</a>, and organized dozens of civic hackathons, Guftugus, Civic Innovation Labs, and community events nationwide. CfP is a registered nonprofit in both Pakistan and the US, and an active member of the global&nbsp;<a href="http://codeforall.org/">Code for All</a> network.</p><p><strong>About the Role</strong></p><p dir="ltr">The Graphic Designer will develop engaging visual materials to support the Digital Skills Training Program. This includes designing infographics, digital assets, and visual aids for curriculum modules and communication materials.</p><p><strong>Key Responsibilities</strong></p><ul><li dir="ltr">Design high-quality visuals for training materials and digital platforms.</li><li dir="ltr">Ensure brand consistency and adherence to Code for Pakistan’s visual guidelines.</li><li dir="ltr">Collaborate with Content Writers and Curriculum Designers to create engaging layouts.</li><li dir="ltr">Develop infographics, icons, and visual learning aids for modules.</li><li dir="ltr">Adapt visuals for multilingual content and accessibility needs.</li></ul><p>What We’re Looking For</p><ul><li dir="ltr">Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design or related discipline.</li><li dir="ltr">3+ years of design experience, preferably in the education or development sector.</li><li dir="ltr">Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite or similar tools.</li><li dir="ltr">Strong visual communication and branding skills.</li></ul>
