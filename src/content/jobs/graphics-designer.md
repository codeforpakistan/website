The Graphic Designer will develop engaging visual materials to support the Digital Skills Training Program. This includes designing infographics, digital assets, and visual aids for curriculum modules and communication materials.

### Key Responsibilities

- Design high-quality visuals for training materials and digital platforms.
- Ensure brand consistency and adherence to Code for Pakistan's visual guidelines.
- Collaborate with Content Writers and Curriculum Designers to create engaging layouts.
- Develop infographics, icons, and visual learning aids for modules.
- Adapt visuals for multilingual content and accessibility needs.

### What We're Looking For

- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design or related discipline.
- 3+ years of design experience, preferably in the education or development sector.
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite or similar tools.
- Strong visual communication and branding skills.
