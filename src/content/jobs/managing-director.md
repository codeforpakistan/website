Code for Pakistan is looking for a high-potential leader and hands-on operator with the drive, judgment, and commitment to grow with the organization over the long term. We are looking for someone with a strong upward trajectory — someone who has already demonstrated unusual ownership, execution, and leadership, and is ready to step into a broader role with real organizational responsibility.

We hope to invest in someone who wants to stay, grow, and take on increasing responsibility as CfP evolves. This role combines three equally important areas of leadership: **business development, delivery, and visibility**. You will help bring in the partnerships and funding that sustain CfP; ensure our programs, products, people, and operations deliver against strategy; and represent CfP credibly with government, funders, partners, media, and the wider civic-tech ecosystem. You will lead a small team closely and directly — setting clear expectations, coaching people, removing barriers, and stepping into the work yourself as needed.

This is a **full-time, permanent position based in Pakistan** — an opportunity to build, lead, and leave a lasting mark on the civic tech ecosystem and its beneficiaries. It is not an advisory, portfolio, or part-time role. You will work in the details alongside the team while also representing CfP and making organization-level decisions.

### How the role is balanced

The MD's time should be roughly balanced across three areas:

* **Business Development** (~1/3): fundraising, donor stewardship, partnerships, paid opportunities, proposals, and institutional relationship-building.
* **Delivery** (~1/3): people management, organizational OKRs, program and project oversight, problem-solving, quality, finances, operations, and ensuring the strategy is actually implemented.
* **Visibility** (~1/3): representing CfP externally, building strategic relationships, supporting thought leadership and communications, and strengthening CfP's visibility and credibility with the audiences that matter.

### Key Responsibilities
* **Lead with Purpose**: Translate Code for Pakistan's mission into clear strategic plans and quarterly goals (OKRs) that deliver measurable social impact across all programs and projects.
* **Lead and Develop a High-Performing Team**: Be the day-to-day people manager for CfP's team. Set clear expectations and measurable OKRs, hold regular one-on-ones and check-ins, coach people through challenges, give direct and constructive feedback, address performance issues early, and help talented team members grow. Build a culture of high ownership, collaboration, accountability, and mutual trust — while improving how the team prioritizes, coordinates, makes decisions, and gets work done so that productivity, efficiency, and quality increase over time.
* **Drive Delivery**: Make sure CfP's strategy turns into high-quality execution. Stay close enough to programs, products, partnerships, finances, MandE, and operations to know what is working, what is stuck, and where intervention is needed. Work with functional leads to solve problems, make tradeoffs, simplify ways of working, maintain momentum, and ensure key commitments deliver meaningful outcomes — not just activities and outputs.
* **Use Evidence to Improve Decisions**: Work with MandE and functional leads to define meaningful outcomes for priority work, review evidence of what changed, and use that learning to decide what CfP should continue, adapt, scale, fund, transfer, or stop.
* **Provide Technology and Product Judgment**: You do not need to be the organization's most technical person, but you should be technologically fluent and comfortable engaging with product, design, data, AI, software, and digital-service questions. Ask good questions, understand tradeoffs, work effectively with technical staff and partners, and help ensure CfP uses technology because it solves a real civic problem — not for technology's sake.
* **Drive Business Development and Fundraising**: Own CfP's business-development and fundraising pipeline across grants, donors, CSR/corporate partnerships, sponsorships, and mission-aligned paid work. Identify and qualify opportunities, build relationships, develop and write proposals and budgets, follow up persistently, move opportunities toward close, and maintain a disciplined CRM/opportunity pipeline. Partner with the Board on introductions, cultivation, and targeted fundraising.
* **Build Strategic Partnerships**: Represent CfP credibly with government, development partners, funders, companies, and civil society. Understand their problems and priorities, identify where CfP can add distinctive value, and help shape opportunities into mission-aligned engagements. Be able to operate strategically in a senior meeting while also understanding what execution would actually require.
* **Build CfP's Visibility and Voice**: Be an active external representative of CfP with government, funders, partners, media, and the civic-tech ecosystem. Work closely with Communications to strengthen CfP's positioning, human stories, thought leadership, and public presence; contribute substantive viewpoints and organizational context; and help turn visibility into relationships, partnerships, funding, and influence.
* **Support Strong Governance**: Serve as the primary operational link between the Board and team, preparing clear information on organizational performance, OKRs, finances, risks, fundraising, and major decisions. Support the Board Chair and Board in governance, compliance, Board development, and effective follow-through.
* **Foster a Learning Culture and Solve Problems Proactively**: Promote innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement, drawing inspiration from global civic tech practices, and anticipate challenges and act decisively to keep projects moving with optimism, creativity, and agility.

### Required Qualifications

* **Leadership and Experience**: Approximately 7–12 years of professional experience, including at least 3 years of direct people-management responsibility, with a track record of increasing scope, ownership, and leadership. We care as much about trajectory, judgment, and demonstrated results as total years of experience. We are especially interested in candidates whose careers show a steep upward trajectory — people who have taken on increasing responsibility quickly, are known for getting difficult things done, and are ready to grow into broader organizational leadership.
* **Strategic and Operational Strength**: Proven ability to plan, execute, and manage operations, programs, and organizational growth.
* **Strategic Range**: Able to move comfortably between levels — discussing strategy, funding, partnerships, and organizational positioning with senior external stakeholders, then turning around and working through implementation details, a proposal, a project blocker, team priorities, or an operating process.
* **Technology Fluency**: Comfortable working in a technology-driven organization and discussing digital products, data, AI, user experience, and technical tradeoffs with practitioners and partners. Technical depth is welcome, but curiosity, judgment, and the ability to learn quickly are more important than being an engineer.
* **Hands-On Team Management and OKRs**: Demonstrated experience setting OKRs (or similar goal-setting frameworks) with individual team members, holding regular check-ins, and coaching people to deliver results.
* **Team Effectiveness and Execution**: Demonstrated ability to make teams more focused, productive, and effective — clarifying priorities, improving coordination and operating rhythms, reducing unnecessary friction, and helping people deliver stronger results with the time and resources available.
* **Fully Committed for the Long Term**: This is a full-time leadership role for someone who wants to build a meaningful chapter of their career at CfP and grow with the organization over time. We are looking for candidates who are genuinely interested in making a sustained commitment to the role — not treating it as a short-term stop before another planned move. CfP should be your primary professional commitment, and you should not maintain another ongoing job, consultancy, business, or substantial professional role that competes with your responsibilities here.
* **Mission-Driven**: Deeply committed to advancing social impact in Pakistan through civic innovation and public service. Prior nonprofit experience is not required, but the candidate should be comfortable operating with the resource discipline, accountability, and public-interest orientation of a mission-driven organization.
* **High Ownership and Growth Mindset**: Ambitious for the mission and eager to grow with the role. Takes initiative without waiting to be asked, follows through relentlessly, learns quickly, seeks feedback, and is energized rather than intimidated by responsibilities that stretch beyond previous experience.
* **Business Development and Fundraising**: Hands-on experience developing and managing grants, budgets, and donor or sponsor relationships, including working with boards or senior leaders on targeted fundraising and sustaining consistent donor follow-up, with demonstrated success advancing or securing support.
* **Relationship Building and Stakeholder Engagement**: Natural connector skilled in building and managing high-level partnerships across government, donors, private sector, and civil society, leveraging networks for collective impact.
* **Analytical Impact Orientation**: Comfortable using data and metrics to measure success and guide strategic decisions.
* **Exceptional Communicator**: Fluent in Urdu and English, with outstanding writing, storytelling, and presentation skills that inspire confidence and collaboration.
* **Judgment, Reliability and Adaptability**: Highly organized, responsive, and dependable; able to prioritize competing demands, make sound decisions with incomplete information, remain steady under pressure, and communicate early when something is at risk.

### Desirable Traits

* Brings an entrepreneurial, resourceful mindset and enjoys building systems as the organization grows.
* Has existing relationships across Pakistan's government, development, technology, philanthropic, or civic sectors — or demonstrates an unusual ability to build them quickly.
* Has experience with product, technology, innovation, startups, civic tech, public-interest technology, or government transformation.
* Brings curiosity, warmth, humility, resilience, and a sense of humor.
