### The Role

**We are hiring 12 Training Coordinators** across three districts: Peshawar, Mardan, and Swabi.

The Training Coordinator will support the planning, logistics, and day-to-day management of the Digital Skills Training Program. This includes scheduling sessions, coordinating trainers, managing participant communications, and ensuring smooth execution of training activities.

### Key Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain detailed training schedules and manage session logistics
- Coordinate between supervisors, participants, and program management
- Manage and oversee the multipurpose center and its daily operations
- Ensure smooth classroom delivery and session management
- Record and maintain daily attendance of participants
- Monitor participant progress throughout the program
- Oversee pre- and post-assessments to measure learning outcomes
- Review and check assessment submissions for accuracy and completeness
- Organize and schedule participant assessment vivas
- Conduct evaluations of participants based on defined criteria
- Address and manage participant queries in a timely manner
- Prepare and compile weekly progress reports
- Support the development of quarterly program reports
- Report regularly to supervisors on activities and progress
- Support procurement and distribution of training materials

### What We're Looking For

- Bachelor's degree in Management, Education, IT or related field
- 3+ years of experience in training logistics or program coordination
- Excellent communication and organizational skills
- Proficiency in MS Office and learning management systems

**Note**: Applications will be considered on a rolling basis.
