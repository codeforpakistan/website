<p>Ever been in a gridlock situation? Pressed for time and stuck in traffic, wishing that you knew an alternate route for your destination? Or ever wanted to register a complaint about illegal activities right on the spot? Ever been in need of nearby rescue personnel to overcome any problems that you might be having while travelling? These are just a few of the issues that citizens have been facing on a daily basis in Peshawar.</p><p>To overcome these issues,&nbsp;<a href="http://www.ptpkp.gov.pk/">Khyber Paktunkhwa Traffic Police</a>&nbsp;(KP Traffic Police) has once again joined hands with the civic hackers of the area to develop a complete solution under the 3rd batch of the&nbsp;<a href="http://codeforpakistan.org/programs/fellowship/">KP Civic Innovation Fellowship Program</a>. Previously, the traffic police has already deployed another solution under the&nbsp;<a href="http://codeforpakistan.org/blog/2016/02/08/the-2015-2016-kp-civic-innovation-fellowship-program/">second batch</a>&nbsp;of the same program to&nbsp;<a href="http://codeforpakistan.org/blog/portfolio-item/kp-traffic/">plot traffic violations onto a heat map</a>&nbsp;which in return has saved millions of rupees, time and efforts of the Traffic Police Department.</p><p>The Traffic Police Department decided to handover the project to the KP Civic Innovation Fellowship Program, which is a joint venture of&nbsp;<a href="http://www.codeforpakistan.org/">Code for Pakistan</a>,&nbsp;<a href="http://kpitb.gov.pk/">Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Information Technology Board (KPITB)</a>&nbsp;and the World Bank. The purpose of the Fellowship program is to bridge the gap between the government and the general public via civic innovation to bring a positive change in society. The development of the project was handed over to one of the&nbsp;<a href="http://codeforpakistan.org/blog/2017/05/22/kp-civic-innovation-fellowship-program-2017/">teams</a>&nbsp;in the Fellowship Program and will be launched before the end of Fellowship cycle in September 2017.</p><figure class="text-bg-light p-3"><blockquote class="blockquote"><p>The Traffic Police Department feels very privileged to work with the KP Civic Innovation Fellowship Program and Code for Pakistan.</p></blockquote><figcaption class="blockquote-footer">Muhammad Aftab, Deputy Director IT, KP Traffic Police</figcaption></figure><p>The android based mobile application “<a href="https://github.com/codeforpakistan/teamKPTraffic17">KP Traffic</a>” is a platform that will facilitate the public as well as the traffic police officials in realtime, improving their existing traditional and inefficient way of registering complaints.</p><p><img class="img-fluid" src="/media/1%2520iajvTlC16dJWADeQCS2cdQ.jpg" data-entity-uuid="10b83047-7a85-4d91-bf5d-5f9ea6accb34" data-entity-type="file"></p><p>This application will solve and simplify&nbsp;<strong>6&nbsp;</strong>traffic related issues including:</p><ul><li><strong>Complaints Module&nbsp;</strong>— Where the public will be able to report complaints about problems, potential traffic violations and corrupt traffic wardens instantly.</li><li><strong>Live Traffic Updates</strong>&nbsp;— The public will be able to check the traffic status in the form of either a busy, congested or a clear road.</li><li><strong>License verification</strong>&nbsp;— This module will help the public to verify their driving license directly through the application in real time.</li><li><strong>Traffic Education</strong>&nbsp;— This module will contain different graphical symbols meant to be used to educate people about traffic rules and regulations.</li><li><strong>Challan Verification</strong>&nbsp;— To overcome corruption in Traffic Police, this module was developed for the public to verify the authenticity of traffic violation tickets.</li><li><strong>Emergency contacts</strong>&nbsp;— This module will allow the public to find a nearby rescue personnel to overcome any problems that they may be facing while travelling.</li></ul>
