Umer Farooq started working on the problem of making education inclusive for the blind community of Pakistan. Umer’s interest in the problem and the deep dive he took resulted in a startup called Boltay Huroof.In a wide-ranging interview about loving the problems to the evolution of the concept, Umer makes a compelling case for participating in a Code for Pakistan Hackathon. Here are SEVEN reasons to participate in a Hackathon:

-   Endorsement of your idea
-   Networking opportunity
-   Accelerate your growth
-   Chance to build a team (nature/skillset/compatibility)
-   Challenge your skillset
-   Building your confidence
-   Prize money to fuel your ideas
