## Course Overview

Master generative AI tools and learn effective prompt engineering techniques to enhance your productivity and learning capabilities.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Understand how Large Language Models (LLMs) work and their capabilities
2. Craft effective prompts for various tasks and applications
3. Apply advanced prompting techniques including zero-shot and few-shot learning
4. Identify and avoid common prompting errors and limitations
5. Use ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI tools effectively across different versions

## Course Modules

### Module 1: Introduction to Generative AI (1 hour)

Explore AI systems that generate content from textual inputs, producing diverse, human-like outputs in various forms.

### Module 2: Introduction to LLMs and its Working (1.5 hours)

Understand Large Language Models (LLMs), which utilize vast data to predict text, enhancing tasks like translation and content generation.

### Module 3: Basics of Prompt Engineering (2 hours)

Delve into Prompt Engineering basics, examine key components of effective prompts, and analyze illustrative examples to enhance model interaction and output control.

### Module 4: Defining a Good Prompt with examples (2 hours)

Learn how to craft effective prompts for AI, enhancing accuracy and relevance with practical examples.

### Module 5: Advanced Prompt with examples (1 hour)

Explore advanced prompt techniques:
* Zero-shot learning
* Few-shot learning
* Chain of thought processes for enhanced model interaction

### Module 6: Prompting Errors and Limitations (1 hour)

Examine common prompting errors and limitations of generative models, focusing on improving accuracy and reducing biases in outputs.

### Module 7: Applications of Prompt Engineering (1 hour)

Discover how prompt engineering enhances AI applications, improving content generation, code development, and customer interactions across various sectors.

### Module 8: Introduction to ChatGPT/Gemini/Deepseek and its Versions (1 hour)

Learn about the features and functionalities of:
* ChatGPT 4.5, o1, and o3-mini
* Key improvements across versions
* How to execute sample prompts across different versions for optimal usage

## Topics Covered

* Introduction to AI tools: ChatGPT, Claude, and other assistants (1 hour)  
* What AI can and cannot do: capabilities and limitations (1 hour)  
* Crafting effective prompts for learning and research (2 hours)  
* Using AI for writing, brainstorming, and problem-solving (1 hour)  
* Fact-checking AI-generated content and ethical AI use (1 hour)

## Notes

* Expected time: 8 modules averaging 1 hour each
* Covers from basic concepts to advanced level prompting
* Focused on GUI versions of Generative models (Code-based examples will also be shared and explained)
* Anyone irrespective of their level of expertise in tech can take this course
* Entry level students are the primary focus
