## Course Overview

Use the Business Model Canvas to map out and validate a service-based business idea.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Apply the Business Model Canvas framework
2. Identify target customers and value propositions
3. Define services and revenue streams
4. "Productize" skills into marketable packages
5. Validate business ideas before investing time

## Topics Covered

* Introduction to the Business Model Canvas (2 hours)  
* Identifying your target customer and value proposition (2 hours)  
* Defining your services and revenue streams (2 hours)  
* "Productizing" your skills into marketable packages (2 hours)
