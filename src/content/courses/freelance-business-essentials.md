## Course Overview

Learn the fundamentals of freelancing, including pricing your services and getting paid.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Calculate appropriate freelance rates
2. Research market rates for your skills
3. Set up international payment systems
4. Create and send professional invoices
5. Manage financial aspects of freelancing

## Topics Covered

* Calculating your freelance rate (hourly vs. project-based) (2 hours)  
* Researching market rates for your skills (2 hours)  
* Setting up payment systems (Payoneer, Wise) (2 hours)  
* Creating and sending professional invoices (2 hours)
