## Course Overview

This course provides participants with a comprehensive understanding of the contemporary workplace environment. Participants will explore workplace culture, organizational structures, professional behavior, digital collaboration tools, and the skills needed to navigate and succeed in modern organizations across various industries.

## Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Understand organizational structures and how modern workplaces operate in the digital age.
2. Navigate workplace culture and adapt to different organizational environments effectively.
3. Demonstrate professional workplace behavior including ethics, etiquette, and digital communication skills.
4. Collaborate effectively using digital tools in diverse, multigenerational, and cross-functional teams.
5. Adapt to future of work trends including remote work, gig economy, and AI integration.
6. Apply change management principles to navigate organizational transformations.
7. Build professional relationships and networks within workplace environments.
8. Manage cloud-based projects and files effectively within team environments.

## Course Plan

### Week 1: Introduction to the Modern Workplace

* Evolution of the workplace: Industrial age to digital age
* Organizational structures: hierarchical, flat, matrix, networked
* Types of modern workplaces: corporate, startups, remote-first companies
* The impact of digital transformation on workplace dynamics

### Week 2: Workplace Culture and Professional Behavior

* Understanding organizational culture and its components
* Professional dress codes and workplace etiquette
* Office politics: navigating diplomatically and ethically
* Building and maintaining positive workplace relationships

### Week 3: Professional Email Writing and Digital Communication

* Email structure: subject lines, greetings, body, closings
* Professional email tone and language
* Email etiquette: response times, CC/BCC usage, attachments
* Common email mistakes to avoid

### Week 4: Virtual Meeting Etiquette and Best Practices

* Preparing for virtual meetings: agenda, technology check, background
* During meetings: muting, video on/off, active participation
* Virtual meeting tools: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet
* Post-meeting follow-up and action items

### Week 5: Cloud File Management and Collaboration

* Google Drive and Shared Drives: organizing, sharing, permissions
* File naming conventions and folder structures
* Version control and collaborative editing
* Best practices for cloud storage security

### Week 6: Managing Online Projects Within Teams

* Project management tools: Asana, Trello, Microsoft Teams, Monday.com
* Creating and assigning tasks within team platforms
* Tracking progress and meeting deadlines digitally
* Team collaboration features: shared calendars, task boards, file sharing

### Week 7: The Future of Work and Digital Workplace Trends

* Remote, hybrid, and flexible work arrangements
* AI and automation's impact on the workplace
* The gig economy and changing employment models
* Skills for the future workplace: adaptability and digital literacy

### Week 8: Building Professional Networks and Career Development

* Building workplace relationships and professional networks
* Mentorship and career progression
* Employee rights, responsibilities, and workplace well-being
* Navigating career transitions professionally

## Topics Covered

* Professional email writing and inbox management (2 hours)  
* Etiquette for virtual meetings (Zoom, Google Meet) (1 hour)  
* Real-time document collaboration with Google Docs & Sheets (2 hours)  
* Cloud file management with Google Drive (1 hour)  
* Creating boards, lists, and cards in Trello to manage projects (2 hours)
