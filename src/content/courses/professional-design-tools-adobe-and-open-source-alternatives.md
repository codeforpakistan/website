## Course Overview

Master industry-standard design tools including Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and their open-source alternatives (GIMP, Inkscape, Krita) to ensure graduates can sustain their practice without prohibitive costs.

**Prerequisite:** Design Fundamentals & Visual Literacy

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Use Adobe Photoshop for photo manipulation and editing
2. Work with GIMP as a Photoshop alternative
3. Create vector graphics with Adobe Illustrator
4. Use Inkscape for vector design
5. Explore digital painting with Krita
6. Navigate software licensing and student options

## Topics Covered

* Adobe Photoshop essentials: layers, masks, and photo manipulation (3 hours)  
* GIMP as a Photoshop alternative: achieving professional results (2 hours)  
* Adobe Illustrator basics: vector graphics and logo design (3 hours)  
* Inkscape for vector design: open-source vector workflow (2 hours)  
* Introduction to Krita for digital painting and illustration (1 hour)  
* Securing student licenses and navigating software costs (1 hour)
