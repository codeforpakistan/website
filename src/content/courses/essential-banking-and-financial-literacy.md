## Course Overview

Understand banking systems, manage personal finances, and use digital financial services safely and effectively.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Open and manage a bank account
2. Understand different account types and banking services
3. Use ATMs, debit cards, and online banking safely
4. Navigate mobile banking apps
5. Create and maintain a personal budget

## Topics Covered

* Opening and managing a bank account (1 hour)  
* Understanding different account types and banking services (1 hour)  
* Using ATMs, debit cards, and online banking safely (2 hours)  
* Mobile banking apps (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bank apps) (2 hours)  
* Creating a personal budget and tracking expenses (2 hours)
