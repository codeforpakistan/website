## Course Overview

Master the art of interviewing with strategies for in-person, phone, and video interviews, using AI to prepare and practice.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Navigate different types of interviews confidently
2. Research companies and roles effectively
3. Prepare answers to common interview questions using AI
4. Apply the STAR method for behavioral responses
5. Set up professional virtual interview environments
6. Demonstrate professional body language and presence
7. Follow up appropriately after interviews

## Topics Covered

* Types of interviews: phone screening, video, panel, and behavioral (1 hour)  
* Researching companies and roles effectively (1 hour)  
* Using AI to prepare answers to common interview questions (2 hours)  
* The STAR method for behavioral interview responses (1 hour)  
* Virtual interview setup: lighting, background, and technical preparation (1 hour)  
* Body language, confidence, and professional presence (1 hour)  
* Asking smart questions and following up after interviews (1 hour)
