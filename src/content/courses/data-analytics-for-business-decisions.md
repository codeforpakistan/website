## Course Overview

Learn to analyze data, identify trends, and create actionable insights to drive business decisions.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Understand data analysis and business intelligence
2. Use advanced Excel functions for analysis
3. Create dashboards and visualizations
4. Work with Power BI or Tableau
5. Interpret data and present insights

## Topics Covered

* Introduction to data analysis and business intelligence (2 hours)  
* Advanced Excel functions for analysis (VLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH) (3 hours)  
* Creating dashboards and visualizations in Excel/Google Sheets (4 hours)  
* Introduction to Power BI or Tableau (4 hours)  
* Interpreting data and presenting insights (2 hours)
