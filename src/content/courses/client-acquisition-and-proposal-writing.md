## Course Overview

Discover how to find clients on platforms like Upwork and Fiverr and write winning proposals.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Create compelling freelance platform profiles
2. Identify and evaluate good client projects
3. Use AI to draft customized proposals
4. Communicate effectively with potential clients
5. Stand out in competitive bidding

## Topics Covered

* Setting up a compelling profile on Upwork or Fiverr (3 hours)  
* Searching for and identifying good client projects (2 hours)  
* Using AI to help draft customized proposals that stand out (3 hours)  
* Best practices for communicating with potential clients (2 hours)
