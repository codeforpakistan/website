## Course Overview

Cultivate entrepreneurial leadership skills and a growth mindset to transition from task-based freelancing to strategic career building and digital entrepreneurship.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Shift from fixed to growth mindset
2. Lead effectively in remote settings
3. Build and lead virtual teams
4. Think entrepreneurially about opportunities
5. Create long-term career vision plans
6. Build your influence through advocacy and self-promotion

## Topics Covered

* Growth mindset vs. fixed mindset: rewiring limiting beliefs (2 hours)  
* Personal leadership in remote settings (2 hours)  
* Building and leading virtual teams and collaborations (2 hours)  
* Entrepreneurial thinking: opportunity recognition and value creation (2 hours)  
* Long-term career visioning and strategic planning (1 hour)  
* Advocacy, self-promotion, and building your influence (1 hour)
