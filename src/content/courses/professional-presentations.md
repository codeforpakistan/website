## Course Overview

Create compelling, visually engaging presentations using PowerPoint or Google Slides.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Apply presentation design principles and storytelling
2. Work with slides, layouts, and master slides
3. Add animations and transitions effectively
4. Incorporate charts, images, and multimedia
5. Deliver presentations confidently with speaker notes

## Topics Covered

* Presentation design principles and storytelling (2 hours)  
* Working with slides, layouts, and master slides (2 hours)  
* Adding animations and transitions effectively (1 hour)  
* Incorporating charts, images, and multimedia (2 hours)  
* Delivery tips and speaker notes (1 hour)
