## Course Overview

Develop the skills and strategies to keep learning throughout your career, staying relevant in a rapidly changing digital economy.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Identify skill gaps and set learning goals
2. Find quality free and paid learning resources
3. Use AI as a personal learning coach and tutor
4. Build sustainable learning habits
5. Document and showcase new skills

## Topics Covered

* Identifying skill gaps and setting learning goals (2 hours)  
* Finding quality free and paid learning resources (Coursera, Udemy, YouTube, LinkedIn Learning) (2 hours)  
* Using AI as a personal learning coach and tutor (2 hours)  
* Building a learning habit and time management (1 hour)  
* Documenting and showcasing new skills (certificates, portfolio updates) (1 hour)
