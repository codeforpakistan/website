## Course Overview

Master the core concepts of digital marketing to promote products, services, or personal brands online.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Understand digital marketing channels
2. Create content marketing strategies
3. Build email marketing campaigns and lists
4. Navigate paid advertising platforms
5. Measure and analyze campaign success

## Topics Covered

* Introduction to digital marketing channels (social, email, search) (2 hours)  
* Content marketing and storytelling (2 hours)  
* Email marketing basics and list building (2 hours)  
* Introduction to paid advertising (Facebook Ads, Google Ads) (2 hours)  
* Analytics and measuring campaign success (2 hours)
