## Course Overview

Develop analytical and creative problem-solving skills that transcend specific tools, fostering cognitive flexibility and strategic thinking essential for the digital economy.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Apply analytical thinking frameworks and mental models
2. Conduct root cause analysis using structured techniques
3. Use creative problem-solving methodologies
4. Make informed decisions under uncertainty
5. Think systematically about complex challenges
6. Leverage AI as a thinking partner

## Topics Covered

* Analytical thinking frameworks and mental models (2 hours)  
* Root cause analysis techniques (5 Whys, Fishbone diagrams) (1 hour)  
* Creative problem-solving methodologies (Design Thinking, SCAMPER) (2 hours)  
* Decision-making under uncertainty and risk assessment (2 hours)  
* Systems thinking for complex challenges (1 hour)  
* Using AI as a thinking partner (prompt engineering for analysis) (2 hours)
