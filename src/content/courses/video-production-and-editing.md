## Course Overview

Learn to create, edit, and produce engaging video content for social media, marketing, and storytelling using accessible tools.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Write effective video scripts and storyboards
2. Shoot professional-quality videos with a smartphone
3. Edit videos using CapCut or DaVinci Resolve
4. Add text, music, and effects
5. Export and optimize videos for different platforms

## Topics Covered

* Video storytelling and scriptwriting basics (2 hours)  
* Smartphone videography: shooting techniques and lighting (2 hours)  
* Video editing with CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (4 hours)  
* Adding text, music, and effects (2 hours)  
* Exporting and optimizing videos for different platforms (2 hours)
