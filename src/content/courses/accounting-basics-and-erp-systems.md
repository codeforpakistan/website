## Course Overview

Learn fundamental accounting principles and how to use Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software for business operations.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Understand accounting fundamentals
2. Record transactions and maintain books
3. Navigate ERP systems effectively
4. Process invoices and manage accounts
5. Generate basic financial reports

## Topics Covered

* Accounting fundamentals: debits, credits, and ledgers (3 hours)  
* Recording transactions and basic bookkeeping (3 hours)  
* Introduction to ERP systems (SAP, QuickBooks, Xero) (3 hours)  
* Invoice processing and accounts payable/receivable (2 hours)  
* Financial reporting basics (1 hour)
