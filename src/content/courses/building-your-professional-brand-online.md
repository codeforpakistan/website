## Course Overview

Create a compelling professional identity online to attract job opportunities through strategic personal branding and digital presence.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Write compelling LinkedIn headlines and "About" sections using AI
2. Optimize your LinkedIn profile with relevant skills and experience
3. Build a simple, elegant one-page portfolio website
4. Showcase your projects, skills, and contact information effectively
5. Maintain a consistent professional brand across platforms

## Topics Covered

* Writing a compelling LinkedIn headline and "About" section using AI (3 hours)  
* Optimizing your LinkedIn profile with skills and experience (2 hours)  
* Building a simple, elegant one-page portfolio with Carrd (4 hours)  
* Showcasing your projects, skills, and contact information (1 hour)
