## Course Overview

Learn design fundamentals and use Canva's AI features to create stunning visuals, with emphasis on strategic design decisions and open-source alternatives.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Apply learned design principles in Canva
2. Use design fundamentals to critique AI-generated outputs
3. Make strategic design decisions, not just use tool features
4. Work with open-source alternatives alongside Canva
5. Create complete brand identity systems

## Topics Covered

* Apply learned design principles in Canva  
* Use design fundamentals to critique AI-generated outputs  
* Focus on strategic design decisions, not just tool proficiency  
* Emphasize open-source alternatives alongside Canva  
* **Capstone Project:** Complete brand identity system (not just random graphics)
