## Course Overview

Develop the critical interpersonal and professional skills that employers value most, including communication, teamwork, and adaptability.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Communicate effectively in verbal and written formats
2. Collaborate successfully in team environments
3. Engage professionally with clients and solve problems
4. Adapt to multicultural and remote work settings
5. Demonstrate strong work ethics and professionalism

## Topics Covered

* Verbal and written communication skills (2 hours)  
* Team collaboration and interpersonal effectiveness (2 hours)  
* Client engagement and problem-solving techniques (2 hours)  
* Adaptability in multicultural and remote work settings (2 hours)  
* Work ethics, professionalism, and workplace etiquette (2 hours)
