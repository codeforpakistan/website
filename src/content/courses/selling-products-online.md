## Course Overview

Learn to set up and manage an online store to sell physical or digital products using popular e-commerce platforms.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Choose the right e-commerce platform
2. Set up online stores and payment methods
3. Create compelling product photography and descriptions
4. Develop pricing strategies and shipping options
5. Market products on social media
6. Handle customer service and orders

## Topics Covered

* Choosing the right platform (Shopify, Etsy, Facebook Marketplace, Daraz) (2 hours)  
* Setting up your online store and payment methods (3 hours)  
* Product photography and description writing with AI (2 hours)  
* Pricing strategies and shipping options (2 hours)  
* Marketing your products on social media (2 hours)  
* Customer service and handling orders (1 hour)
