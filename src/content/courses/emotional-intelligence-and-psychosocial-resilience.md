## Course Overview

Build emotional strength and resilience to navigate the psychological challenges of remote work, freelancing, and operating in male-dominated digital spaces, with culturally sensitive strategies for the KP context.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Practice self-awareness and emotional regulation techniques
2. Manage rejection and failure in the gig economy
3. Apply stress management techniques for remote work
4. Build confidence in male-dominated digital spaces
5. Navigate family expectations and cultural sensitivity
6. Implement trauma-informed self-care strategies

## Topics Covered

* Self-awareness and emotional regulation techniques (2 hours)  
* Managing rejection and failure in the gig economy (2 hours)  
* Stress management techniques for remote work (2 hours)  
* Building confidence in male-dominated digital spaces (2 hours)  
* Dealing with imposter syndrome and self-doubt (1 hour)  
* Cultural sensitivity and navigating family expectations (2 hours)  
* Trauma-informed self-care strategies and boundary setting (1 hour)
