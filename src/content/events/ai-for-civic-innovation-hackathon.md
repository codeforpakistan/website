Code for Pakistan is excited to launch the **AI for Civic Innovation Hackathon** in collaboration with [**FAST NUCES Islamabad**](https://www.nu.edu.pk?utm_source=chatgpt.com), [**Grey Software**](https://gsoftware.org?utm_source=chatgpt.com), and [**Scrimba**](https://scrimba.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com). The hackathon will bring together students, developers, designers, AI practitioners, and civic innovators to build AI-powered solutions that address real-world civic challenges.

## Thematic Areas

The hackathon focuses on developing technology and AI-driven solutions across three thematic areas:

### Democracy, Inclusion & Civic Participation

Build solutions that strengthen citizen engagement, civic education, voter accessibility, and public participation.

**Example ideas:**

* Voter awareness and election information tools  
* Youth civic participation platforms  
* Civic education chatbots  
* Women-focused civic engagement and political awareness tools

### Technology for Civic Good

Develop technology solutions that address public and community challenges while improving access to information, services, participation, and safety.

**Example ideas:**

* Healthcare access tools  
* Disaster response systems  
* Air quality monitoring platforms  
* Fact-checking and misinformation awareness tools  
* Journalist safety and digital security solutions

### Open Data & Access to Information

Create solutions that make public information more accessible, transparent, and useful for citizens.

**Example ideas:**

* Open data dashboards  
* Budget transparency tools  
* Information accessibility systems  
* Fact-checking tools

## Prizes

## Participation

Participants will work in teams of up to **four members** to design and prototype solutions that create meaningful civic impact. Whether you're a student, developer, designer, researcher, or AI enthusiast, this is an opportunity to apply your skills to challenges that matter.

Join us in building technology that serves communities and creates a better tomorrow.
