Shahzeb Khan is a technology professional specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and the development of AI-powered solutions. At Code for Pakistan, he leads the development of AI-driven civic technology solutions, working with government, public, and private sector partners to identify challenges faced by citizens and develop technology-enabled solutions that address them.

With extensive experience in Artificial Intelligence, Shahzeb’s expertise spans Natural Language Understanding (NLU), Natural Language Generation (NLG), Large Language Models (LLMs), machine learning, and conversational AI. His work includes developing locally deployable chatbots, exploring language technologies for low-resource environments, and building practical AI applications that improve accessibility and user experiences.

Alongside his work at Code for Pakistan, Shahzeb serves as a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at FAST-NUCES, Peshawar Campus, where he teaches courses in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Knowledge Representation and Reasoning. He also supervises research-driven and industry-oriented student projects, several of which have received government and institutional support.

Prior to joining Code for Pakistan, Shahzeb led AI engineering teams and worked on AI research and development initiatives, including large language models, voice recognition, and AI-based solutions for clients. He holds a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from FAST-NUCES, Islamabad, and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Islamia College, Peshawar.

Beyond technology and research, Shahzeb is passionate about community development and believes in using knowledge and innovation to create meaningful social impact.
