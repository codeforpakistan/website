<p>Komal Ansar is a results-driven Program Associate at Code for Pakistan, dedicated to civic engagement and women's empowerment in tech and governance. She oversees the Civic Innovator Program and the KP Women Civic Internship Program, designing critical training initiatives that integrate civic technology for impactful solutions.</p><p>Her background includes significant contributions as a Monitoring and Evaluation Officer for the World Food Program and a Coordinator for the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO). Komal's expertise in program coordination and training was honed as a Training Assistant for the World Bank's Pakistan Community Support Program.</p><p>A proven event organizer, Komal has successfully led the Android Seekho Workshop, Hult Prize competition, KP Business Expo, and the Women Online Safety Hackathon. Her commitment extends to community service, organizing various philanthropic efforts.</p><p>Komal earned an MBA in Finance and a BBA in Finance from the Institute of Management Studies, University of Peshawar.</p>
