Mahhah Qayyum is a dynamic communications leader with over six years of experience in strategic communications, digital marketing, and media management. Currently serving as **Senior Manager Communications** at *Media Matters (Grayling Affiliate)*, she oversees corporate communications and executive visibility for major clients such as Jazz and Telenor Pakistan. Her work blends creativity and strategic insight—driving measurable gains in brand visibility, stakeholder engagement, and public perception.

Mahhah has led cross-functional teams in managing end-to-end campaigns, crisis communication, and digital transformation initiatives. She previously held leadership roles at *Symmetry Group*, where she supported digital strategy for top national brands, and contributed to content and brand strategy in the real estate and print sectors.

Armed with a **BS in Mass Communication (Multimedia Journalism)** and an **MS in Peace & Conflict Studies** from NUST Islamabad, Mahhah combines analytical depth with storytelling acumen. Recognized for her reliability, creativity, and collaborative approach, she continues to build meaningful narratives that elevate brands and shape public dialogue.
