Dania began her journey in civic technology as a Communications Associate at Code for Pakistan, where she has passionately embraced this new field. With a background in corporate and advertising sectors, she adapted quickly, bringing a strong commitment to social innovation and SDGs, especially in gender equality and education. Her dedication and curiosity have empowered her to overcome the learning curve in civic tech.

Bringing a fresh, creative approach to Code for Pakistan’s social media, she has effectively crafted social media campaigns that resonate with diverse audiences. Her strategic insights and adaptability ensure that each post aligns with the organization’s mission, reaching diverse audiences effectively.

Beyond work, Dania’s passion for media and culture fuels her creativity. She enjoys exploring new films, diving into music, experimenting with photography, and researching trends, drawing endless inspiration that she channels back into her work.
