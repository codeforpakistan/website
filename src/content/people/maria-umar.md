<p><strong>Maria Umar</strong> is the Managing Director of <strong>Code for Pakistan</strong>, a civic tech organization dedicated to making governance more citizen-centric through technology. Widely recognized as a pioneer in digital empowerment, Maria is also the founder and president of the <strong>Women’s Digital League (WDL)</strong>—Pakistan’s first fully virtual platform that trains and connects women with digital work opportunities.</p><p>As an <strong>Ashoka Changemaker</strong>, Maria’s innovative work has earned international recognition. Under her leadership, WDL has won the Early Stage Award in Changemaker’s “Women Powering Work” competition and was featured in a national campaign by Google Pakistan for its groundbreaking use of technology. The platform has also partnered with the <strong>World Bank</strong> to implement a digital literacy program in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, as well as collaborated with <strong>Facebook</strong> and <strong>UpWork</strong> to further its mission of economic empowerment for women.</p><p>Maria’s impact has been spotlighted by leading global media including <strong>Forbes</strong> and <strong>CNBC</strong>, and she is featured as a case study in Alec Ross’s bestselling book, <em>Industries of the Future</em>, for her work enabling women to thrive in the digital economy.</p><p>In addition to her entrepreneurial achievements, Maria has served as a <strong>Gender and Inclusion Specialist</strong> in the fintech sector, promoting financial inclusion and equitable access to technology. Her contributions to gender equity in tech were celebrated at the <strong>Singapore Tech Festival</strong>, where she joined a fireside chat with <strong>Melinda French Gates</strong>.</p>
