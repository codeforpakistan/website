Eman brings her expertise in software project management, community coordination, and leadership. With a degree in software engineering, Eman has held roles as a Software Project Manager and Project Coordinator, successfully leading cross-functional teams and managing complex projects to successful outcomes. Her background equips her with robust organizational, communication, and strategic planning skills essential for effective stakeholder engagement.

Passionate about using technology for social impact, Eman previously served as Lead for Google Developer Student Clubs, empowering students to create tech-driven solutions for community needs. Under her leadership, students explored Google technologies, innovated, and tackled real-world challenges in an inclusive environment. As a Gold Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador, Eman also mentored a global student network, spearheading initiatives that connected technology and community empowerment.

At Code for Pakistan, Eman combines her project management skills with her dedication to fostering civic engagement. She is committed to building inclusive spaces where people can connect, learn, and collaborate to drive positive social change.
