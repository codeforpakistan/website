Farooq Islam is a talented developer with growing expertise in JavaScript, full-stack, and mobile application development. His expanding portfolio showcases his proficiency in crafting interactive web applications with intuitive and responsive user interfaces.

As a technology associate at Code For Pakistan, Farooq excels at merging his front-end and back-end development expertise with his strong collaborative skills. He effectively works with diverse teams to translate user requirements into functional applications. His skill set extends to mobile app development, notably with Dart, applied effectively to the HCAI app, which was developed in collaboration with the World Health Organization (WHO), the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), and in partnership with Code for Pakistan.

During his early career as a React Developer, Farooq consistently delivered high-quality solutions. He utilized a wide array of technologies to enhance user experiences, including React, Context API, Firebase, Material UI, React Tables, React Calendar, React Select, and Smart Banners. His projects ranged from managing large datasets with React Tables to implementing intuitive scheduling with React Calendar. Farooq's meticulous attention to detail and commitment to best practices ensured the delivery of exceptional solutions.

Among his notable projects is the Forkify App, a user-friendly web application tailored for recipe searching. With features such as a search function, pagination, and bookmarking, the app provides users with a seamless experience of exploring and saving recipes. Farooq's dedication to continuous improvement drives him to adapt to the latest industry trends and tools, ensuring the delivery of exceptional digital experiences across both web and mobile platforms.

Farooq hails from the scenic Mattani region of KP, renowned for its hospitable people and fertile agricultural land. He holds a bachelor's degree in Information Technology from the University of Agriculture, Peshawar.
