<p>Fasi Zaka is a prominent media personality, writer, and public intellectual recognized for his sharp political insight and pioneering use of satire in Pakistani journalism. With a career spanning over two decades across television, radio, and print, he has shaped national discourse on governance, radicalization, and civic responsibility. A Rhodes Scholar from the University of Oxford, Zaka brings a blend of academic depth and media fluency to every platform he engages with.</p><p>Fasi first gained public attention as the host of <em>News, Views &amp; Confused</em> on AAJ TV, one of Pakistan’s earliest political satire shows. He later expanded his reach through <em>The Fasi Zaka &amp; Friends Show</em> on FM91, cultivating a reputation as one of the country’s most influential radio voices. As co-host of <em>Do Raaye</em> on Dawn News, he continues to dissect Pakistan’s social and political complexities with clarity, humor, and empathy.</p><p>Beyond broadcasting, Zaka has contributed to development and policy sectors, focusing on communication strategies, governance reform, and counter-extremism initiatives. His writings, featured in national and international publications and in works like <em>Rethinking Pakistan: A 21st Century Perspective</em>, bridge satire with substantive analysis, challenging audiences to think critically about the country’s future.</p><p>Recognized as a Young Global Leader by the World Economic Forum, Fasi Zaka embodies a rare mix of intellect, authenticity, and civic commitment. His voice remains central to Pakistan’s ongoing conversation about democracy, media ethics, and the role of informed citizenry in shaping national progress.</p>
