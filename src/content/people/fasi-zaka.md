Fasi Zaka is a prominent media personality, writer, and public intellectual recognized for his sharp political insight and pioneering use of satire in Pakistani journalism. With a career spanning over two decades across television, radio, and print, he has shaped national discourse on governance, radicalization, and civic responsibility. A Rhodes Scholar from the University of Oxford, Zaka brings a blend of academic depth and media fluency to every platform he engages with.

Fasi first gained public attention as the host of *News, Views & Confused* on AAJ TV, one of Pakistan’s earliest political satire shows. He later expanded his reach through *The Fasi Zaka & Friends Show* on FM91, cultivating a reputation as one of the country’s most influential radio voices. As co-host of *Do Raaye* on Dawn News, he continues to dissect Pakistan’s social and political complexities with clarity, humor, and empathy.

Beyond broadcasting, Zaka has contributed to development and policy sectors, focusing on communication strategies, governance reform, and counter-extremism initiatives. His writings, featured in national and international publications and in works like *Rethinking Pakistan: A 21st Century Perspective*, bridge satire with substantive analysis, challenging audiences to think critically about the country’s future.

Recognized as a Young Global Leader by the World Economic Forum, Fasi Zaka embodies a rare mix of intellect, authenticity, and civic commitment. His voice remains central to Pakistan’s ongoing conversation about democracy, media ethics, and the role of informed citizenry in shaping national progress.
