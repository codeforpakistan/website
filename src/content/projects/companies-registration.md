An ICT Companies Registration Portal was designed for Startups, IT services companies, and Freelancers to register themselves with the KPITB. The mission behind this portal was to collect information on companies, startups, and freelancers operating in the KP province and provide them with public sector and private sector opportunities and development projects so that they can grow, scale, and contribute to the local economy and boost employment.

The portal was launched under the 6th cycle of the KP Fellowship Program.
