A Fellowship Team from our Fellowship 2015 cycle, partnered with PDMA (Emergency Wing) created a new website and content management system for PDMA to convey information to NGOs, journalists, and the public working in disaster-affected areas so as to provide more swift relief to disaster-stricken residents.

Compared to the department’s previous web-application, this site is dynamic. With better user-forms, dynamic content & easy-to-update methods which can be managed very easily by any person without having any knowledge of HTML. Thus anyone from the staff can easily maintain the public content.
