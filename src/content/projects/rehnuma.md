<p>&nbsp;</p><p>Inspired by Code for America’s Honolulu Answers, Rehnuma was developed under the first cycle of the KP Government Innovation Fellowship Program in 2014, as a solution for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government with a new approach to make it easier for people to navigate city information and services quickly.</p><p><br>Rehnuma was a citizen-focused website that offered FAQs around government services with a clean, and easy-to-navigate design. The content was organised based on citizen understanding, with the aim to answer questions in the simplest way possible.&nbsp;</p><p><br>Rehnuma was designed to be very user-friendly. It decluttered the govt website experience, and it solved a problem people ordinarily have. Although no longer active the open source code can be reused to launch similar initiatives around the world.&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;</p>
