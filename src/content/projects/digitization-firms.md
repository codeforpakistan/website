A team of Fellows helped develop an online system for Directorate of Industries and Commerce to digitise their manual records. This sped up the registration process and made it easy for the Directorate employees to search and sort information about any firm/society/NGO and madrassa.

The online system consisted of different modules through which the department could easily digitise their data. The team also set up a local secure network system with backup and alternative power systems. Additionally, the team developed an intelligent reporting system for the Directorate.
