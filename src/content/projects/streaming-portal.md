An online streaming and polling portal was developed for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Provincial Assembly, responsible for legislation in the province. The entity recognized the importance of bridging the gap between legislators and the public. They reached out to the team for assistance in developing a portal that would enable the public to stream live proceedings at the assembly or watch them later. This portal also facilitated the public to provide feedback on legislators' discussions during different sessions, participate in various polls on petitions submitted by legislators, and make their voices heard.
