<p>Crowd Durshal was a crowdsourcing platform developed for startups operating in KP. The platform included various features, such as the ability for startups to create their profiles, find investors, and apply for different funding opportunities. The platform was developed under the 3rd cycle of the KP Fellowship Program.&nbsp;</p><p>Crowd Durshal’s sole purpose was to connect tech companies with the best investment opportunities to convert them into sustainable businesses. The project was also aimed to enhance the existing startup ecosystem by providing an opportunity to network with the startup community via building a strong portfolio on Crowd Durshal's website.</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
