<p>&nbsp;</p><p>The Directorate General of Sports Khyber Pakhtunkhwa plays a crucial role in promoting sports activities throughout the province, including providing training to players and coaches, issuing membership cards for sports enthusiasts, and organising trials and events for athletes across the region. &nbsp;</p><p>A team of Fellows under the 7th cycle worked closely with the Directorate General of Sports. The team developed a new website featuring different pages highlighting sporting facilities and events organised by the department. Additionally, they worked on implementing an online membership registration system and a sports and trial management system for the department.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
