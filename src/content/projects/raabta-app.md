<p dir="ltr">&nbsp;</p><p dir="ltr">In 2018 under the fourth Fellowship cycle, the&nbsp;<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kptrafficpolice.trafficapp&amp;hl=en&amp;gl=US">Raabta App</a> was developed in collaboration with the KP Traffic Police Department as a digitisation initiative. The App offers numerous features like the ability to verify licenses, traffic education, live updates on traffic conditions and Challan tracking. Since its launch in 2018 the app has been downloaded over 50,000 times and has an active and growing user base.&nbsp;</p><p dir="ltr">The app’s code is available on GitHub and can be used for similar traffic related initiatives globally.</p>
