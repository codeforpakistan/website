<p>&nbsp;</p><p>As part of the E-governance initiatives of the KP Fellowship Program, the digitisation of firms, industries, NGOs, and madrassas was undertaken by the Fellows during the 4th cycle of the KP Fellowship Program. Through this digital intervention, online web portals were created to facilitate ease of doing business and allow firms, industries, NGOs, and madrassas to easily register digitally.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
