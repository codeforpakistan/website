The Fellowship Team from our Fellowship 2015 cycle partnered with the Provincial Disaster Management Authority (PDMA) to improve the department’s service delivery process. They developed an online tool to bring efficiency to the process of provisioning relief funds to the needy by keeping records of casualties, cattle damage, and distributions in disaster-affected areas.
