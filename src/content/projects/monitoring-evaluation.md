<p><br>The KPITB Monitoring and Evaluation Cell required a web-based Monitoring and Evaluation System to streamline M&amp;E activities and reporting within the department.</p><p>A team of Fellows, as part of the 7th Fellowship cycle, developed an online system tailored to the department's needs. This system aimed to streamline the data collection process for monitoring and evaluating programs and projects operational within KPITB.</p>
