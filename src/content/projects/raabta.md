The Raabta app, developed in collaboration with the KP Traffic Police Department, provides live traffic updates, driving license verification, traffic ticket verification, driving license test preparation, traffic education, and real-time warden deployment tracking.

The app was originally developed under the 3rd cycle of the KP Fellowship Program and subsequently updated during the 4th and 5th cycles.

The Raabta app has been a resounding success for both CfP and the KP Traffic Police Department. Since its launch in 2017, the app has received over 50,000 downloads and facilitates thousands of user requests on a daily basis.
