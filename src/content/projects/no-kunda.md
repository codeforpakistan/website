<p><br>NoKunda was an open-source, cross-platform smartphone application aimed at reporting and curbing electricity theft in Pakistan.</p><p>Developed as a civic initiative under the banner of Code for Pakistan and KP IT Board, it can also be used in any other country where the menace of power theft exists. The aim of the civic initiative was to use technology to solve our daily life problems and to encourage governments to support the general public in coming up with their own solutions for the common good.</p><p>&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;</p>
